## Assessment

- The varied wording and self-samples did what they targeted: the multi-input suite rose from 2-3/18 to 8/18 with
  every answer admitted. The remaining failures are the two conditional families.
- Retention is unchanged from `one_2e5` (all gate probes pass), and held-out documentation/Q&A loss improved slightly.
- Retrieval training helped only modestly (10.93 → 10.56 with context; the relative gain from context is unchanged at
  about 11%). A 512-token context leaves little room for reference text.
- The frozen Sol tests and dev lexical matches slipped a little (155 → 142; 23 → 17). Single-seed differences of this
  size need a second seed before being called regressions.
- No model yet says "no change needed" correctly (0/24); Stage 3 managed 6/24.

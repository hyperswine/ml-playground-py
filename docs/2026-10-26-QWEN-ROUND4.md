## What changed from round 3

Round 3's same-question closed-book pairs (each retrieval question repeated without its reference) were replaced by
3,000 *different* training-partition questions with no reference (`qos round2 closed`; none appears in any Stage 5 or
round-2 retrieval example; [report](data/qos_ai_benchmark/qwen_round4_20261026/closed_book_report.json)). Everything
else is as in round 3: steps before the answer (36,946 Stage 5 answers and 678 round-2 rows rewritten), the round-2
rows, the 3,000 long-reference retrieval examples, seed 7, same recipe. 92,314 examples, 999k added target tokens;
38 minutes of training.

## Assessment

- **Round 4 is the best Sol model so far**: 324/360 frozen tests, 222/249 stated outputs, the highest strict-trace
  score (92/120) and the first model to solve all three `branch_max` tasks. Steps first carries the gain; replacing
  the same-question pairs kept it.
- **Closed-book recall is kept** (43/90, as round 3 and seed 7), and both retrieval perplexities are the best
  measured (5.54 without and 3.22 with references), so different-question closed-book data did not teach the model to
  ignore references the way same-question pairs did (round 3: 3.35).
- **The lexical score with references does not agree** (18/51, against round 2's 28). It is a regex rubric over 51
  greedy answers from single training runs; perplexity over 300 held-out answers is the steadier measure. Whether
  round 2's 28 reproduces would need a second round-2 seed. Until then, round 2 is not clearly better at using
  references, and round 4 is better at everything else.
- **Recommendation**: use round 4 as the reference Qwen model for further work. No default model has been changed.

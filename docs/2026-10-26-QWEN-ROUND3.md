## What changed from round 2

Same data (Stage 5 mixture, round-2 rows, 3,000 long-reference retrieval examples), recipe and seed. Two changes,
applied in `qos qwen_sft merge` (`--steps-first --closed-book-pairs`):

1. **Steps before the answer.** Every answer of the form `It prints X. Steps: S.` became `Steps: S.` followed by
   `It prints X.` on its own line: 36,946 Stage 5 answers and 678 round-2 rows. The old order makes the model commit
   to X before it works anything out; in the fresh-question test its own trace often reached the right value while
   the headline was wrong. All scorers accept the new order (they read `It prints X.` at the end of the answer, and
   trace fields as `name=value` in any order).
2. **Closed-book pairs.** Each retrieval example also appears without its reference, with the same answer (3,000
   more examples), meant to keep answering from memory after round 2's drop.

92,314 examples, 1.0M added target tokens; 2,885 updates at 6,380 tokens/s, 36 minutes of training.

**Install fix.** `flash-linear-attention` 0.6.0 (released 2026-10-23) needs a newer Triton than the image's, and the
job installed the package unpinned. The import check failed within a minute and the pod stopped itself. The job now
pins 0.5.2 (what earlier runs used) and logs the versions: transformers 5.18.0.dev0, fla 0.5.2, Triton 3.4.0,
torch 2.8.0.

## Assessment

- **Steps first is the largest single improvement since the move to Qwen**: +31 frozen tests (289 → 321), stated
  outputs 198 → 220, and the best results so far on combinations (42/48) and the fresh probe (26/32). It cost no new
  data, only reordering existing verified answers. Execution remains a safety net (358/360 either way).
- **Closed-book pairs did their stated job but cancelled round 2's gain.** Closed-book development Q&A is back to
  seed 7's level (43), but with references the model answers 16 of 51 instead of round 2's 28. Training on the same
  question with and without the reference, with the same answer, teaches that the reference is optional.
- Round 3 is the best Sol model; round 2 remains the best at answering from references. No default model has been
  changed.

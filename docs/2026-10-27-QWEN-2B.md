# Qwen3.5-2B on the round-4 data

Created: 2026-10-27T05:30:00+11:00 (Australia/Sydney). Kind: experiment report. Follows
[round 4](2026-10-26-QWEN-ROUND4.md). GPU: one Secure RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition (96 GB) in EU-RO-1
(pod `e5faqrnzte3k62`, terminated), 72 minutes at $2.49/hr, about $3.00 with the CPU read pod. Retrieval
measurements ran on the M1. Raw reports: `data/qos_ai_benchmark/qwen2b_round4_20261026/`.

## Setup

- **Model**: Qwen3.5-2B (2.27B parameters; same tokenizer and model class as the 0.8B, verified by tokenizer hash),
  full fine-tune with the vision tower frozen, fp32 weights and AdamW states with bf16 autocast.
- **Data and recipe**: exactly round 4's (Stage 5 mixture with steps first, round-2 rows, 3,000 long-reference
  retrieval examples, 3,000 different closed-book questions; 92,314 examples), seed 7, lr 1e-5, 32 examples per
  update, one pass of 2,885 updates.
- **Efficiency**: the 96 GB card allowed micro-batches of up to 8,192 padded and 4,096 answer tokens
  (`TRAIN_FLAGS='--token-budget 8192 --target-budget 4096'`; same updates as the 0.8B runs). 4,720 tokens/s, GPU 99%
  busy, 47 of 98 GB; 46 minutes of training. Base-model answers were skipped. Weights (4.4 GB bf16) were pulled to the
  M1 with 12 streams in about 7 minutes.

## Assessment

- **Size helps on almost everything.** With the same data and recipe, the 2B adds 15 frozen Sol tests (324 → 339),
  solves the whole multi-input suite (18/18, every one of the 108 cases), raises the strict trace score from 92 to 109
  of 120, and answers 9 more development questions from memory (43 → 52) and 12 more as `ask` would answer them
  (50 → 62). Retrieval perplexity drops by about 13% with and without references.
- Small regressions: repair-with-output on the original benchmark (40 → 35) and the probe (26 → 25). These are
  single runs; the frozen repair stratum (39/39) and program writing improved.
- **Cost of using it**: on the M1 (16 GB) the 2B runs in fp32 with 17% of memory free; development answers with
  references took 19 minutes (0.8B: 10) and the retrieval measurement about three hours (0.8B: 50 minutes).
- The 2B round-4 model is the strongest Sol model in this project. No default model has been changed.
